# Chart Types

The library automatically selects the best visualization for your dataset based on its shape — number of dimensions, time series presence, and value characteristics. You can also force a specific chart type via `config.chartType`. Below is a reference of all available chart types.

---

### Key Figure

Displays a single prominent number. **Best for:** datasets where all dimensions have exactly one selected value, resulting in a single data point.

<Canvas of={KeyFigureStories.Default} />

---

### Line

Shows trends over time or ordinal sequences. **Best for:** time series data with up to 10 series.

<Canvas of={LineStories.Default} />

---

### Vertical Bar

Compares values across a single categorical dimension. **Best for:** one dimension with categorical or time data.

<Canvas of={VerticalBarStories.Default} />

---

### Grouped Vertical Bar

Compares values across two dimensions side by side. **Best for:** two dimensions (up to 20 categories × 4 groups), with time or ordinal axis.

<Canvas of={GroupedVerticalBarStories.Default} />

---

### Stacked Vertical Bar

Shows composition of values across two dimensions. **Best for:** two dimensions with non-negative data, showing part-to-whole relationships over time.

<Canvas of={StackedVerticalBarStories.Default} />

---

### Percent Vertical Bar

Shows proportional composition normalized to 100%. **Best for:** same as stacked bar, emphasizing relative proportions rather than absolute values.

<Canvas of={PercentBarStories.Vertical} />

---

### Horizontal Bar

Compares values with categories on the Y axis. **Best for:** single categorical dimension at a single point in time (no time axis).

<Canvas of={HorizontalBarStories.Default} />

---

### Grouped Horizontal Bar

Side-by-side comparison with horizontal orientation. **Best for:** two dimensions without a time axis, with up to 20 categories × 4 groups.

<Canvas of={GroupedHorizontalBarStories.Default} />

---

### Stacked Horizontal Bar

Composition view with horizontal orientation. **Best for:** two non-negative dimensions without a time axis, showing part-to-whole.

<Canvas of={StackedHorizontalBarStories.Default} />

---

### Percent Horizontal Bar

Proportional composition normalized to 100% horizontally. **Best for:** same as stacked horizontal, emphasizing relative proportions.

<Canvas of={PercentBarStories.Horizontal} />

---

### Pie

Shows proportional composition as circular segments. **Best for:** single dimension with 2–10 non-negative categories at a single point in time.

<Canvas of={PieStories.Default} />

---

### Scatter Plot

Plots two measures against each other as points. **Best for:** datasets with exactly 2 content/metric values per observation.

<Canvas of={ScatterPlotStories.Default} />

---

### Pyramid

Back-to-back horizontal bars for comparing two groups along an ordinal dimension. **Best for:** one ordinal dimension (e.g. age groups) split by a binary dimension (e.g. male/female).

<Canvas of={PyramidStories.Default} />

---

### Map

Choropleth map showing geographic distribution. **Best for:** datasets with a geographic dimension at a single point in time, when map geometry is available via `mapProvider`.

<Canvas of={MapStories.Regions} />

---

### Table

N-dimensional pivot table as a fallback or explicit choice. **Best for:** any dataset structure — serves as the universal fallback when no visual chart type is applicable.

<Canvas of={TablesStories.Table} />
